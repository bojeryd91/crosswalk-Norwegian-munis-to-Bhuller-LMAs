## Attribution of source material

The data are derived from material published by **Statistics Norway (SSB)**
under CC BY 4.0 (<https://www.ssb.no/en/diverse/lisens>):

- Municipality codes, names and their changes over time: SSB Klass,
  classification 131 (Standard for kommuneinndeling),
  <https://www.ssb.no/klass/klassifikasjoner/131>.
- Labor market regions: Bhuller, M. S. (2009). *Inndeling av Norge i
  arbeidsmarkedsregioner*. Notater 2009/24, Statistisk sentralbyrå.
  <https://www.ssb.no/arbeid-og-lonn/artikler-og-publikasjoner/inndeling-av-norge-i-arbeidsmarkedsregioner>

**Changes made:** the SSB material was transcribed and compiled into
crosswalks between classification vintages, and the labor market regions
were carried from the 2008 classification to the other vintages. Where a
merger combined municipalities from different regions, a single region was
assigned by hand (see the README). These changes are the author's and are
not endorsed by Statistics Norway.
